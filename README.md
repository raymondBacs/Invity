Invity is a modern, all-in-one online invitation and event-sharing platform designed for any occasion — from personal celebrations to corporate gatherings.
With Invity, users can easily create, customize, and send invitations online while tracking RSVPs, managing guests, and integrating with social media platforms for seamless sharing.
Built for scalability and flexibility, Invity is designed to integrate with other business tools and projects, making it ideal for developers, event organizers, and digital platforms looking to add a plug-and-play invitation system.
